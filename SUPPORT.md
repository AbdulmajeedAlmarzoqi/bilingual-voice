# Support

## Common questions

**The voice I want is not listed.**
iOS runs third-party speech engines in a restricted process that can only reach voices whose
data ships inside iOS. Enhanced and Premium voices you downloaded, Siri voices, and voices from
other apps are invisible to every third-party engine, not just this one. The app lists what it
can use and explains, next to each one it cannot, why not. There is no workaround.

**VoiceOver is not using the engine.**
Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → Speech → Voice, then pick your profile under Bilingual
Voice. That is the only step in VoiceOver.

**My profile does not appear in that list.**
Open the app once and wait about half a minute. iOS reloads third-party voices in the
background and it is not instant.

**My trial ended.**
The paid features stop and VoiceOver goes back to the system's default voice. Unlock the app
from the About screen, or tap Restore Purchases if you already bought it.

**I bought it on another device.**
About → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
