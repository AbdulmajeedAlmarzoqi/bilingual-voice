# Support

## Common questions

**How do I get the beta?**
Open https://testflight.apple.com/join/KZUs8RRm on your iPhone, install TestFlight if you do not
have it, and accept the invitation. TestFlight keeps BiVoice up to date. Purchases in the beta
are not charged.

**VoiceOver is not using BiVoice.**
Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → Speech → Voice, then choose your profile under BiVoice.
That is the only step in Settings. You do not need to add a second language to VoiceOver, and
Detect Languages can stay off.

**My profile does not appear in that list.**
Open BiVoice and wait about half a minute; iOS loads third-party voices in the background.

**BiVoice says Restart Required.**
iOS has stopped noticing newly installed speech engines, which can happen after BiVoice is
deleted and installed again, or updated. Only a restart clears it: restart your iPhone, then
open BiVoice. After the restart, BiVoice appears in VoiceOver's voice list by itself, even
before you unlock.

**Punctuation is read even though VoiceOver's punctuation is set to None, or not read when it is
set to All.**
VoiceOver does not pass its punctuation setting to other speech engines. Set it in BiVoice
instead: open your profile, then Speech Rules → Read Punctuation, and choose None, Some or All.

**Numbers, commas or symbols are read by the wrong voice.**
Open your profile and look at Speech Rules. Numbers, Punctuation and Symbols can each follow the
text before them, the text after them, or your primary language.

**One word in another language is read by the wrong voice.**
That is Change Voice for Single Words in Speech Rules. Turn it on to give every word its own
language's voice, or off to keep a single word with the sentence around it.

**One voice is louder, faster or higher than the other.**
Open your profile, choose the language, and adjust its Speaking Rate, Pitch and Volume. They are
relative to what VoiceOver asks for, so VoiceOver's own speaking rate still applies to both.

**The voice I want is not listed.**
iOS runs third-party speech engines in a restricted process that can only reach voices whose
data ships inside iOS. Enhanced and Premium voices you downloaded, Siri voices, Eloquence, and
voices from other apps are unavailable to every third-party engine, not just this one. The app
lists what it can use and explains, next to each one it cannot, why not. There is no
workaround.

**Switching between profiles.**
Add Voice to the VoiceOver rotor (Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → Rotor), then turn the
rotor to Voice while reading and swipe up or down.

**My trial ended.**
Your settings stop applying and VoiceOver goes back to the system's voice; nothing else changes.
Purchase BiVoice from the About screen, or tap Restore Purchases there if you already bought
it.

**I bought it on another device.**
About → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Useful to include: your iPhone model, your iOS version, the BiVoice build number (shown in
TestFlight), which languages and voices you set up, and what you expected to happen instead.

Please do not send text you would not want to share. BiVoice never records what it speaks, so I
